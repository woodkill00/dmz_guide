# Community conduct

Help people learn. Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people. Welcome newcomers, communicate clearly, respect accessibility needs, and give constructive reviews.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation, spam and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to disclose personal information or continue an unwanted interaction.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close conversations, or restrict participation proportionately. Explain moderation decisions when safe, allow a response, and avoid retaliating against good-faith reports. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not adjudicate it alone where another reviewer is available.

A confidential conduct-reporting channel has not yet been established. Do not publish sensitive reports in issues. Ask a maintainer to provide a verified private route without disclosing incident details. Before opening additional community spaces, the owner must assign moderation coverage and a private reporting route. No response-time guarantee is made.
