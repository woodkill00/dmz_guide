# Proposed requirements-quality checklist
Reviewer-owned; unchecked because no formal owner ratification has occurred. These are not implementation tests.
- [ ] Title/build/season and screenshot provenance are distinguished.
- [ ] Every in-scope user outcome has FR/NFR and A-ID coverage.
- [ ] Currency, requirements, gates, effects and inventory pools have unambiguous semantics.
- [ ] Now/later policy and unsupported recipes are explicit.
- [ ] Picture-pack portability includes assets or clearly declares reference-only limitations.
- [ ] Content rights and contribution governance have owners.
- [ ] No accounts, Discord tokens or public publication are implied by private editing.
- [ ] Mobile/no-JS guide/map-list/error states are specified.
- [ ] Test thresholds have documented profiles and are not reported as passed.
- [ ] Content versions, migration, revocation and local-state recovery are specified.
- [ ] Remaining questions are scoped to affected slices, not hidden assumptions.
