# COD Central Orders completion

## Outcome
All 24 Hajin and five X01 Orders chapter rosters have source-reported tasks and page rewards. Individual Order pages show available location notes and a direct link to the matching COD Central chapter for its full tips and screenshots.

## Requirements
- **FR-001:** Fill the two disputed Hajin task lists from COD Central while retaining visible disagreement and current-build uncertainty.
- **FR-002:** Show all five X01 page reward lists in the directory and each X01 Order detail as source reports, without granting inventory or progress.
- **FR-003:** Link each individual Order to its COD Central chapter; present concise paraphrased location notes where useful, with no copied provider images or long prose.
- **FR-004:** Preserve existing Order IDs, roster navigation, local progress, spoiler handling, and page audit links.
- **FR-005:** Audit all 87 source mission entries (81 Orders and six story missions) against local identity/task coverage and run runtime, catalog, and build checks.

## Boundaries
Source reports are not proof of live game state. Provider disagreements remain visible. Provider screenshots stay at the linked source page.
