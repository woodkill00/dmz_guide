## FR008 — unfinished collection and crafting states (2026-10-31)
Extend Needed to indirect ingredients of unfinished upgrades and missions, never completed roots. Default scope for the unanswered optional mission question is all unfinished missions; no speculative mission quantities. Existing Next/All columns remain upgrade-only. A separate labelled Collection status explains direct/recipe demand and known quantities. Yellow identifies unresolved quantities/routes; red means a known positive shortage; green means all listed quantity demand is covered. A separate Craft tag identifies indirect ingredients in every state. Crafting relevance remains labelled even when red/green. No color-only information.
Use declared safe same-scope recipe inputs/output quantities and explicit selected routes, or the sole producing recipe. Ambiguous alternatives, unknown evidence/costs/stock, cycles and work limits cannot become green. Aggregate shared consumed input demand once across recipe chains and retain tools by maximum; use saved/draft stock after reserves, batch yields and stock already held in intermediate outputs. No actual craft, mission completion, stock mutation, route auto-save or inferred recipe/unlock. Explain quantity coverage separately from craft eligibility. Safe candidate references may remain yellow when exact accounting is unavailable; completion reprojects membership. Preserve draft focus and bounded50-row rendering. Test shared chains, alternatives, completed roots, held outputs, reserves, unknowns, privacy, cycles, UI colors/text, Save/Cancel and completion.

FR008 refinements: Needed now means unfinished upgrade/mission collection membership only; arbitrary selected-goal-only or completed-root requirements no longer enter this filter. Existing selected-goal quantity columns remain separate. Mission tracks match the existing item-use contract: dmz-order,dmz-story,mission,campaign; completed/unlocked excludes a mission, other states remain unfinished. Campaign contributors are labelled. No mission aliases or amounts are invented. Color priority is known positive shortage red, then unresolved/partial yellow, then complete known quantity coverage green; a separate Crafting tag identifies indirect use in every state. Graph is bounded by admitted20000records and500000node/edge work, no recursive call stack. Aggregate each shared item's incoming demand before batch rounding and stock allocation. Hidden recipes do not establish a sole producer: if any recipes are privacy-withheld, automatic route selection is disabled for the projection; an explicit visible route remains usable. Hidden identities/alternative counts are never exposed. Cycles/limits cannot report green.
