US1 Assess evidence and track favorites/visited/checked/revisit without converting possible source spawns into active-match facts.
US2 Review duplicate candidates side by side on map; Same location, Keep separate, Review later, undo/reset; capture-bound decisions become stale on source changes and never erase evidence.
US3 Use compact gameplay controls, favorite categories/saved searches, density grouping/overlap expansion, and copy safe view links.
US4 Inspect new/moved/renamed/missing source records before explicit update application.

Edit/import/preview road centerlines and junctions, support partial networks and show disconnected/off-road connectors honestly.

## Assumptions / clarified decisions
Road-first planning, not live navigation; geometric shortest on known roads. No road topology exists in retained POI/raster captures. Author/import network with explicit junctions; starter local trace from existing authorized imagery is partial/unverified. Road geometry is not inferred from POI positions. Unknown bridges/gates/vehicle access remain unknown. Public shared links exclude private saved searches. Game observations are owner assertions with evidence, not objective truth.
