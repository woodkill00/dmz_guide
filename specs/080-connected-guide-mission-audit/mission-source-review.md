## Hajin Order detail admission — 2026-11-14

The 66 linked Order detail pages in [DMZTracker's Orders directory](https://www.dmztracker.gg/orders/) were fetched and compared by identity with the 66 non-Story rows in [COD Central's Orders article](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-orders/). All 66 identities matched after retaining explicit aliases and repeated-name variants: `Jail Break`/`Jailbreak`, Healer on page 7 versus page 10, and No Time in The Grid versus page 23. The original three page-one records remain; 63 additional `observed` Order records each carry a direct detail-page source ID and the COD Central article source ID. Each new record has empty effects, prerequisites and item requirements with `requirementsKnown: false`. Current-build behavior, rewards and unlock timing remain unverified.

Short objective labels were admitted from the direct detail pages for 61 of the 63 new records. The Vault uses the broader shared steps because the tracker adds an uncorroborated card/code sequence. DRC uses the shared locker action without adopting the article's uncorroborated location list. Astronaut and Stalking the Stalker have no admitted steps: the providers disagree on objective counts or requirements. Their mission audit rows visibly request step review. Ears Everywhere also differs in displayed step count, but both sources agree on the broadcast-system hack and Encrypted Voice Logs; the tracker presents those as two steps while the article includes entering the area as a third action. Only the two shared core steps are recorded.

Fixed pages 1, 4, 7, 10, 11, 14 and 15 have 21 Order records with `orderPage`. The other 45 Orders retain their reported chapter/page context in the description but no fixed `orderPage`, since chapter selection and late-page order can alter placement. The 24-slot Hajin audit therefore shows seven recorded fixed rosters and 17 unassigned slots, not 17 missing Order identities. Six Story Mission records remain separate. The official starter contains 182 objectives total: 66 reported Orders, six reported Story Missions and 110 pre-existing records. X01 Orders were not part of this Hajin pass and need their own page-set model and source review.
