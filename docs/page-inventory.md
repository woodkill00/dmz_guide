# Page inventory and mobile journeys

Home / mission control: search, plan next upgrade, resume selected goal, recent verified updates. Do not call it a live game dashboard.
Items: searchable catalog -> item detail -> needed-for recipes/goals -> next action and locations; provenance and unknown fields visible.
Crafting / FOB upgrades: categories, requirements, unlocks, typed effects, compare owned/missing and add to plan. Ready state depends on actual local inputs and trusted gates.
Plan: goals, strict-priority ledger, now/later needs, protected reserves, projected batch surplus and explicit mode. Change quantity with accessible controls and undo.
Map and list: common filters, floors, selected entity, directions, confidence, provenance and goal links. Missing imagery retains list utility.
Orders / DMZ story / Campaign: distinct taxonomy and progression namespaces, answer-first walkthrough, prerequisites, spoiler-aware index, sequence and related locations. No invented walkthroughs to fill gaps.
Progress: completed milestones and unlocks distinct from desired goals and bookmarks. Reset scope and manual game-state assumptions visible.
Settings / data editor: theme, local profiles, storage/backup status, guided content editing, import diff, export scope, pack version, conflicts and asset rights.
Community: verified Discord destination, contribution instructions, moderation policy, sources/credits and correction status. Read without joining.

Core task tests: (1) Find a named material and why to keep it. (2) Pick an upgrade and determine missing quantity. (3) See which future goal shares an item without double-counting. (4) Reach a location from a goal with the map unavailable. (5) Correct a recipe locally and transfer a pack with pictures to a clean profile. Test first-use and return-use on a phone; observe completion errors, taps and comprehension, not just preference ratings.

Scope labels: S1-S3 essential usable core; S4-S7 required full requested product roadmap; S8 optional reliability/sync expansion. Launch scope may be chosen by owner, but unfinished requested features remain visibly tracked, not described as complete.
