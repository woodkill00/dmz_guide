# Product roadmap

Status: planned. Repository setup is enabling work, not completion of any player-facing milestone. The detailed baseline is [feature 001](../specs/001-guide-foundation/spec.md); keep its requirement and acceptance IDs traceable when creating implementation slices.

1. **Evidence and contracts:** establish game/build scope, rights, portable schemas and a mobile visual prototype.
2. **Readable guide:** accessible search and item/recipe details with sources, uncertainty and meaningful empty states.
3. **First useful plan:** local inventory, one selected goal, shortages/currency/gates and backup/recovery. Then shared multi-goal planning with priorities, batches and now/later distinctions.
4. **Safe content editing:** guided private edits, versioned JSON, previewed imports and bounded, self-contained picture packs.
5. **Location intelligence:** original/licensed imagery, calibrated coordinates, floors, sources and map/list parity.
6. **Progression and guides:** distinct progression, Orders/story/campaign categories, spoilers and verified prerequisites.
7. **Community and release operations:** verified community destination, corrections, moderation and launch evidence.

Maps, editing, progression/story and community remain part of the requested product. Choosing a smaller first release does not remove those commitments. Optional offline downloads, accounts, cloud sync, bots and optimization require separate assessment and approval.

## Decisions before launch

Owner: target game/build and first-release scope; hosting/domain/budget; named content and moderation owners; verified community destination. Content reviewers: record coverage and asset rights. Engineering/review: actual acceptance, security, accessibility, mobile and performance evidence.

Do not assign dates or claim “complete” until scope, capacity and evidence justify it. Track dependencies and unresolved decisions in each owning specification.
