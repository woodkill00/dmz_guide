YOUR LOCAL WORKSPACE

How to use your map

Find locations, compare sources and build your own DMZ field guide from gameplay.

The buttons below open the relevant tools. They do not run updates, change filters or save anything. Help opens separately from the map so your current draft stays in place. When working in multiple map tabs, save changes in one at a time.

Routes, tracking and gameplay planner

Open Planner. Add waypoints from a marker popup or search-result popup, or pick a map point in Routes. Use the visible Routes button on the map to return to your saved route. Drag a numbered stop using its handle, or use Up / Down with keyboard or touch. Click a numbered map stop or use Mark next reached to record manual progress; Show next centers the next resolved stop. Reset progress has Undo until another saved change or reload. The selected route and reached stops survive reload/private backup; progress follows the location when reordered, and reselecting a changed source stop clears its old reached flag. This is manual tracking, not live in-game GPS. Reverse, remove or save the ordered stops; the route redraws automatically.

Follow known roads uses the current connected road network. Stops snap to the nearest open road within160image pixels. Solid lines follow its geometry; dashed connectors are off-road. Missing, stale, disconnected or distant stops show a leg error rather than a fabricated road. Direct mode draws explicitly labelled straight lines. Lengths are canonical image pixels, not meters, ETA or a safe route.

Road network lets you trace X,Y vertices or pick them on the map, connect endpoints within6pixels, inspect/block/delete segments and set direction. Preview a bounded road JSON file before importing. Interior crossings do not connect unless a shared junction is authored. The optional local starter is a partial visual trace, unverified and stored separately from your backup. Your imported/authored graph is backed up. Observe actual roads, bridges, gates and vehicle access during gameplay before relying on them.

Tracking records favorites, visited/checked/revisit and your observation evidence/date. Owner-confirmed requires evidence and a date; provider spawn claims remain unchanged. Capture changes make observations and source-bound route stops stale until you explicitly review/reselect them. Duplicate queue compares compatible names and nearby coordinates side by side, highlights both, and keeps reversible Same / Separate / Later decisions bound to the exact captures.

Gameplay gives compact saved-search and favorite-category shortcuts, a favorite-location view and larger icons. Density groups crowded visible markers into selectable member lists; turn it off to restore individual markers. Share view previews a link before applying public website/category masks and position; private notes, queries, points, routes and screenshots stay out of links. Missing/unknown categories disable Apply. Source update previews now list renamed, moved, new and missing records before existing Apply.

1. Start here

  1. Open Map key and choose the websites and marker categories you want.
  2. Find a location by name, category, source, notes or evidence. Open its details to see its source.
  3. Add your own POI, or right-click a location on the map to start a draft there.
  4. Create an icon and assign it to a point or a category.
  5. Download a private backup after a useful editing session.

The map displays local captures and your own saved work. It does not track a live match. Imported locations are source claims, not automatically verified game facts.

2. Move around & read coordinates

Drag to pan. Scroll, pinch or use the + / − buttons to zoom. Reset view fits the whole terrain. The north arrow points up. Fullscreen is available when your browser supports it.

Focus the map to pan with arrow keys, zoom with + / −, and reset with R. Tab moves between controls. Escape closes a dialog; the map key also closes with Escape while focused inside it.

The coordinate grid labels cells such as G6. Its A–J / 1–9 lines are aligned to the captured terrain. The bottom bar separately shows image X/Y positions, measured from the top-left of the 4096 × 4096 image. Grid references and image pixels are different. Unlabelled margins show a dash. Alignment is source-based, not independent verification of every in-game location.

3. Choose layers in Map key

Map key opens the legend directly. Website layers each have a main switch and category sublayers. Choose all, none or a mix; hiding a website retains its category selection. A website name links to its original map.

Each category shows its source icon, name and record count. Empty categories can remain listed without inventing markers. My points and Reviewed map have separate visibility switches. Search and layer masks can both affect what is drawn.

Sources & Layers provides another view of these controls. Settings are saved in this browser when storage is available. Visibility is separate from deleting or changing a source capture.

Some MapGenie entries are mission or equipment reference cards placed on its reference board, outside the terrain. They remain searchable with details, but have no terrain pin or Jump destination. A record count can therefore exceed the number of pins.

4. Understand duplicate markers

The viewer compares both the marker name and map position, along with compatible category, known floor and game build. Nearby markers with different names are kept separate. Unknown categories or conflicting information can also stay separate for review.

Likely copies are marked with an asterisk and may appear greyed out in category rows. One selected source draws the pin, preferring the official source when it is enabled. Turning it off lets another selected source represent the location. The source records remain intact.

Use matching-marker details to inspect the comparison. Show duplicate markers lets you inspect every copy. A keep-separate decision applies to the reviewed capture; changed source data may need checking again. These are conservative suggestions, not proof that all sources agree.

5. Check for source updates

  1. Open Map key and choose Update map sources.
  2. Wait for each site's result. Inspect added, changed or missing entries, category changes and icon notes.
  3. Choose Apply source update to keep the preview, or dismiss it.

Checks need the owner-enabled local update service and configured sources. Normal map viewing and reading this guide do not collect new source data. If a site fails or its terrain alignment changes, the last good capture stays available. “Missing” means absent from that feed, not confirmed removed from the game.

Unchanged content reuses the existing capture rather than appending duplicate records. Older captures are retained as evidence. Duplicate display checks also run across selected sources; source imports are not merged into your own POIs.

If applying was interrupted after the workspace saved, use Resume pending update. It retries activation without importing the same capture again. Manage staged updates lets you inspect or dismiss abandoned previews. If a newer update superseded one, follow the on-screen recovery explanation and run a fresh check. Avoid clearing browser storage as a recovery shortcut.

6. Add your own POIs, locations & marks

Right-click inside the terrain to open an unsaved point at that position. Alternatively, open My points, enter image X/Y, or choose Pick position on map. On touch devices, use the form or map-pick button.

  1. Enter a title, kind and category. Categories help organize your own discoveries.
  2. Add notes, evidence, floor, build and verification state as appropriate. Leave unknown facts unknown.
  3. Select an icon override, or use the category default.
  4. Confirm the content is yours or authorized, then save.

Add an observation date and expand Owned screenshots / gameplay evidence to attach a captioned screenshot. Files up to8MiB /8192px /32Mi pixels are normalized privately to PNG up to1600px /2MiB; a detailed image may be reduced further to fit. Keep original full-resolution evidence separately. Up to4 screenshots per point and32 /8MiB total fit within the existing private-backup budget.

Choose Route / line or Shaded area under Geometry to create your own annotation. Enter one X,Y vertex per line, or pick a map position and choose Add current X/Y vertex. Routes need at least2 distinct vertices; areas need3 noncollinear vertices. Choose a color, save, then edit or undo using the normal point controls. At most200 vertices per shape,500 shapes and20000 total vertices are allowed. The shape's search location is its vertex-average anchor; its line length/perimeter is measured in image pixels.

Use a saved point's edit controls to change it. Delete removes that private point; Undo can restore the previous saved workspace state. Save or explicitly cancel a current draft before changing sections or starting another point. Right-click does not silently replace an existing draft.

7. Create & reuse custom icons

In Icons, choose a General, Loot cache, Medical, Danger, Objective or Extraction starter. Change its frame, symbol, colors, border width, symbol size and rotation. An optional 1–3 letter/number label replaces the symbol. Enter an accessible description.

The large preview and small previews on dark/light backgrounds show how your design reads at map size. Choose Save icon to library, then select it in a point's Icon override or use Set category icon on its library card.

Edit a copy opens saved settings for a new variant without changing icons already used by points. Identical pixels reuse a library entry. A matching raster-only image can gain editable settings while keeping its existing identity and references.

Designer Undo / Redo keeps up to50 edit states. My saved design starters reuses existing recipes. Export an editable icon pack to move up to32 recipes as JSON; import previews validate and render the pack before one library save. Identical generated images reuse existing entries. Raster-only images are not part of these recipe packs.

Download SVG exports a scalable vector; Download PNG exports a transparent image. Your editable designs also travel in private backups. Existing PNG/JPEG/WebP uploads use a separate form. Only one icon form is editable at a time: save or cancel before switching. Creation uses built-in shapes; arbitrary SVG uploads and freehand path editing are not supported.

8. Find, compare & flag locations

Find locations searches loaded source entries, your points and reviewed locations by their available names, categories and source names, plus notes, screenshot captions and gameplay evidence. A hidden result can still be inspected. Jump highlights a placed result without changing your saved masks. Reference cards cannot be jumped to.

Use quotes for a phrase, or combine words. Open the advanced controls for source, category, record type, floor/build, visibility, placement and verification. Approximate title matches can be included; matching website copies can be grouped or expanded without deleting them. Choose a result title or Jump to highlight its real location. ArrowDown from the search box focuses the first Jump button; Up/Down moves between result Jump buttons.

Marker popups keep the title and action buttons visible while long details scroll. Focus the details panel to scroll with arrow keys or Page Down; Escape closes the popup. Notes preserve line breaks. Nearby searches from a selected result. You can also capture the current map center or enter a grid cell / image X,Y, set an optional radius and sort nearest-first. Results show grid cells and image-pixel distances. A standalone G6 query limits results to that cell; near:G6 finds nearby matches. Try near:G6 clinic, source:mapgenie has:location, floor:2 or is:unverified. These are map distances, not live player tracking, meters or safe-route instructions.

Completeness check compares provider records against the loaded official data. It distinguishes matches, uncertain differences, unplaced entries and provider-only claims. A missing category, bounded comparison or unloaded source cannot establish complete game coverage.

My discrepancies records your own notes about source differences and whether you have resolved them. It does not submit anything to the provider or contact other users. Changed capture identities can make earlier notes or decisions inactive rather than silently transferring them to another location.

9. Save a useful map view

Set your website masters, category mix, source search and My points / Reviewed switches, then open Saved views and name the current view. Apply it later to restore that combination. Review any missing-category notice after source updates.

A saved view stores visibility preferences, not a separate set of POIs. Temporary search highlights and duplicate-inspection mode are not included.

10. Inspect sources & build the reviewed map

Sources shows imported snapshots, original records, category mappings and coordinate registration. Snapshots preserve what a website supplied; they are separate from your own observations. Unregistered frames remain unplotted until their alignment is reviewed.

For a local source file, preview its changes before importing. Coordinate calibration is an advanced workflow: use identifiable terrain controls and separate check points, inspect the reported fit, and accept only a reviewed alignment. Do not reuse a different site's image scale or guess positions.

Review lets you inspect source records and choose the title, position and category for a reviewed entity. Authored corrections need your evidence and declaration. Accepting a review does not erase the raw source claims. Source changes can mark a review stale; inspect and reconfirm it. Split and undo controls retain an explainable decision trail.

11. Compare another background

In Sources, choose a local raster background you own or are authorized to use, describe it and confirm authorization. Choose Preview background, review the image, then Keep local background. On its saved card, choose Calibrate background, enter identifiable terrain controls and separate check points, choose Validate calibration, review the result, then Accept registration. Only then use the comparison background and opacity controls in Map key / Layers to compare it with the canonical terrain.

Markers keep their canonical coordinates while the registered overlay is shown. A comparison image is not a new map coordinate system. Background image files are separate backup dependencies: keep copies of the original local files as well as your workspace backup.

12. Back up & recover your work

Open Workspace health & recovery to inspect storage headroom, capture age, protected cleanup links and recovery dependencies. Choose Read recent update history for a local, read-only list of completed, cancelled and waiting checks; it does not collect source data.

Your points, icons, designs, notes, preferences and review decisions are local to this browser profile. This is not cloud sync. Clearing site data, changing profiles or losing the device can lose work.

  1. Choose Download private backup in Backups and confirm the file actually saved.
  2. To restore, choose a private backup and preview the replacement.
  3. Download the current backup first, then explicitly confirm replacement. Inspect any missing-dependency notices.

Private backups preserve owned points and icons, including editable icon designs. Restricted source data is omitted with dependency notices; a restored reference may need its local source capture again. Background pixels are separate files. No public/share export is provided.

A failed save preserves the last valid workspace and retains the attempted draft for retry. Export a retained unsaved draft before reloading saved state. Keep a copy before restoring or starting a new workspace. A reminder appears after enough edits or time with changes; the browser can record only that a download started, not that you kept the file.

14. Troubleshooting & limits

I cannot see a marker.
Check the website master, its category mask, search, My points / Reviewed visibility and duplicate mode. Reference cards have no terrain pin. A failed source retains its last good data where available.
The terrain does not load.
Use Retry map. This local app needs separately supplied map imagery; the app does not distribute it. Help and workspace tools can still be opened.
Updates are unavailable.
The local update service or source configuration may not be enabled. Read its status; a link from this page does not start or enable collection.
A save conflicts with another tab.
Keep the draft, export it if needed, and reload the newer saved workspace before reconciling. Save from one map tab at a time.
What does Health show?
Storage headroom, capture ages, protected snapshot-pruning links, update controls and a recovery-dependency manifest. Check background dependencies to see which local images need reattaching. Browser storage estimates are advisory, and this tool does not automatically delete filesystem captures that a different profile or backup may need.
How much can I keep?
The icon library holds up to32 icons /8MiB. Private workspaces allow up to5000 custom points,20 source snapshots,20000 retained source records and20MiB of backup content. Source captures allow up to5000 entries each; combined source viewing/search has a20000-entry budget. At a limit, back up and review old material; excess sources can remain retained but unavailable for display until you select a combination that fits.

Unsaved work is guarded when switching sections. Use Save or Cancel draft / preview intentionally. Opening a tool here does not import, erase or approve anything.
